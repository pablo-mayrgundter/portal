NetGL + deck.gl

A deck.gl GlobeView host. The dots and the orbit are a three.js guest in an iframe from another origin, replayed into deck's WebGL2 context after deck's layers, in deck's clip space. The rings are deck's own layer at the same points: a dot centred in its ring is alignment. The guest depth-tests against deck's globe, so the orbit's far half is hidden, and Tokyo and Sydney, on the far side, draw nothing.
