Door Earth in place

WASD + drag. A Cesium globe through a portal door in a three.js room. Cesium runs in a hidden iframe; its WebGL calls cross over postMessage and execute in this page's GL context.

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. The Earth is a Cesium globe composited into a three.js scene with the host's stars, sun, and moon — depth works both ways as the moon passes. Guest lag: – frames.
