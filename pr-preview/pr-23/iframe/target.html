Iframe portal target

This page is the destination world for the iframe-portal demo. Open the host page (index.html) to see it composited through a portal.

After traversal it becomes the visible "here" — its display canvas takes over and WASD/mouse drive a camera in worldB.

WASD + drag mouse. You're inside worldB now — the portal behind you shows worldA, served by the host page over postMessage. Walk back through the door to return.
